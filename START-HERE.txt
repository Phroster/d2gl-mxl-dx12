MXL SMOOTH MOTION 1.0
Helps Median XL feel smoother online.

COPY, PASTE, PLAY

1. Open the Median XL launcher and let it finish updating.
   Choose Glide or DirectDraw.
   Turn on Unofficial Graphics Drivers for that choice.
   Turn Windowed off.

2. Close the game and launcher.

3. Copy these FIVE files into your Median XL game folder, next to Game.exe:
      glide3x.dll
      ddraw.dll
      d2gl.mpq
      d2gl.ini
      d2fps.ini
   Choose Replace.

4. Start the game and play. The smoothing fix turns on by itself.

That's it. No settings to edit and no installer to run.
Leave the existing d2fps.dll alone - the game still needs it.

Already have custom settings? Back up your files first.
Copying the INIs replaces those settings with ours.
Read docs/INSTALL.md if you want to keep your settings instead.

CHECK IT OR TWEAK IT

Press Ctrl+O. Look for: Multiplayer Smoothing Fix: On
Use that same menu to change the picture.
Want a window? Press Alt+Enter after starting.
Keep Windowed off in the launcher.

For Median XL 2.14.0 / Diablo II 1.13c on Windows 10+.
