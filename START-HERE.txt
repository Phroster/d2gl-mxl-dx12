MXL SMOOTH MOTION DX12 1.1
Smoother gameplay and sharper visuals for Median XL.

NEW IN 1.1
Smoother crowded fights and automatic map reveal when you enter an act.
The reveal may briefly pause on entry; pressing T afterward should be instant.
Performance recording is off by default. The fixes stay on.

COPY, PASTE, PLAY

1. Open the Median XL launcher and let updates finish.
   Choose Glide or DirectDraw. Turn Windowed off.
   Under Unofficial Graphic Drivers, tick BOTH:
      Glide3x.dll (D2GL, D2DX)
      Ddraw.dll (D2GL, CNC-DDRAW)

2. Close the game and launcher.

3. Copy these SIX files into your game folder, next to Game.exe:
      glide3x.dll
      ddraw.dll
      d2gl.mpq
      d2gl.ini
      d2fps.ini
      mxl-diagnostics.ini
   Choose Replace.

4. Start playing. The smoothing fix turns on by itself.

Leave the official d2fps.dll alone - the game still needs it.
Back up your files first if you have custom graphics or FPS settings.

CTRL+O
Change graphics and check Multiplayer Smoothing Fix: On.
The FPS tab changes FPS and movement settings.
Save FPS changes, then restart.
Alt+Enter switches fullscreen/windowed mode.

RESHADE
Choose DirectX 10/11/12 in the ReShade installer.
Keep your existing preset and shader folder.
Switch an old OpenGL ReShade installation to DirectX too.
ReShade is optional and is not included in this ZIP.

For Windows 10+, a DX12-capable GPU and supported MXL 2.14.0 / Diablo II 1.13c.
