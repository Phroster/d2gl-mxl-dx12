MXL SMOOTH MOTION DX12 1.0
Smoother gameplay and sharper visuals for Median XL.

Smooth movement in single player and online, steadier crowded fights,
HD text and cursor, automatic map reveal and DirectX ReShade support.

INSTALL IN FOUR STEPS

1. Open the Median XL launcher and let updates finish.
   Choose Glide or DirectDraw. Turn Windowed off.
   Under Unofficial Graphic Drivers, tick BOTH:
      Glide3x.dll (D2GL, D2DX)
      Ddraw.dll (D2GL, CNC-DDRAW)

2. Close the game and launcher. Extract the downloaded ZIP.

3. Copy these SIX files into your game folder, beside Game.exe:
      glide3x.dll
      ddraw.dll
      d2gl.mpq
      d2gl.ini
      d2fps.ini
      mxl-diagnostics.ini
   Choose Replace. Back up your custom settings first.

4. Launch Median XL. Movement smoothing and automatic map reveal are ready to use.

Keep the existing d2fps.dll. The game needs it.

UPGRADING?
Replace glide3x.dll, ddraw.dll and mxl-diagnostics.ini.
Keep your graphics/FPS INIs and matching MPQ to preserve your setup.

MAKE IT YOURS
Ctrl+O: graphics options and Multiplayer Smoothing Fix status.
Ctrl+O > FPS: FPS limit and movement settings. Save and restart.
Alt+Enter: fullscreen or windowed mode.

Your FPS target follows your monitor by default.
Preparing a new act's map can briefly pause the game on entry.
Pressing T afterward does not repeat the full reveal.

RESHADE
Select Game.exe and DirectX 10/11/12 in the ReShade installer.
Keep your preset and shader folder. Switch old OpenGL setups to DirectX.
ReShade is optional and downloaded separately.

REQUIREMENTS
Median XL 2.14.0 / Diablo II: Lord of Destruction 1.13c.
Windows 10 or newer and a DirectX 12 graphics card.
